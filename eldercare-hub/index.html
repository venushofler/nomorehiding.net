About

About the Eldercare Resource Hub™

The mission of no more hiding™ is to be a trusted resource for women navigating life's transitions. Many women struggle to live whole and live free because they are balancing the demands of their own lives, careers, and relationships while also caring for aging loved ones.

Making decisions about an aging loved one's care can be overwhelming, especially when families face unfamiliar medical terms, changing care needs, financial considerations, and difficult choices. Providing hands-on care brings additional responsibilities, practical challenges, and decisions that can affect the caregiver's own health, relationships, finances, and daily life.

The Eldercare Resource Hub™ (ERH) is a new initiative of no more hiding, LLC, created to support family caregivers as well as the loved ones receiving care.

The Hub will provide practical information, trusted resources, caregiving tips, information about available caregiver benefits and perks, as well as templates and tools to help families make informed elder care decisions. Just as importantly, it will offer resources to help caregivers recognize their own needs, understand their options, and manage their caregiving responsibilities.

The Hub will be an independent online resource that families can access directly or through nomorehiding.net.

Our mission: Make elder care information and resources easier to find, understand, and use—for both the person receiving care and the person providing it.

What to expect

What Will You Find at the Eldercare Resource Hub™?

The Hub is being designed to offer:

Trusted Resources

Links to government agencies, established organizations, and other reliable sources of elder care information.

Practical Tools

Checklists, worksheets, and planning resources to help families organize information and prepare for important decisions.

Articles and Insights

Plain-language information about caregiving challenges, care options, and lessons learned.

Caregiving Guidance

Resources organized around a seven-phase approach to assessing needs, developing plans, managing care, and adapting as circumstances change.

The Eldercare Resource Hub™ is being developed with family caregivers in mind, whether they are preparing ahead or responding to an unexpected change in a loved one's health or living situation.

Introducing

What Do I Do Now?

A Plain-Language Workbook for Family Caregivers Facing Elder Care Decisions

Written by Renetta Smith, MBA, CP, MPM, APM
Founder & CEO, no more hiding™, LLC

Anticipated Publication: 2027

Drawing on her professional background in project management and personal experiences navigating elder care, Renetta developed What Do I Do Now? to help family caregivers assess their circumstances, understand their options, and take practical steps toward informed decisions.

Readers will be encouraged to:

  • Assess their loved one's current needs and available support.
  • Understand care options and identify important questions to ask.
  • Organize information and develop a family elder care plan.
  • Prepare for hospitalizations, rehabilitation, and care transitions.
  • Monitor changing needs and adjust plans when necessary.
  • Recognize and address the needs and capacity of family caregivers.

Rather than telling families what decisions to make, the workbook helps them understand their situations, consider their options, and determine their next steps.

The Eldercare Resource Hub™ and What Do I Do Now? are designed to work together.

The workbook provides a practical planning framework, while the Hub will offer access to additional information and resources.

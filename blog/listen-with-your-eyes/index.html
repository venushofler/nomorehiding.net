Listen With Your Eyes…and Believe What You See

People will show you who they are. Pay attention—not with your ears, but with your eyes.

Renetta Smith, Founder & CEO, no more hiding™ · September 25, 2026 ·

We want to believe the people who say they love and care about us. Sometimes, we close our eyes to what they consistently show us and keep listening to what they say. But words without action eventually lose their weight.

When someone really loves and cares about you, they listen when you speak, they listen to the tone in which you speak and watch carefully when you don’t speak…they are watchful of your needs…and you’ll never have to beg or remind them.

Be it bestie, partner, spouse, relative, spiritual mentor, supervisor, clergy—doesn’t matter.

Sometimes we keep our eyes closed because opening them would force us to admit that the relationship we’ve been fighting to manage and maintain may not actually be the relationship we have. (Let that rest in your spirit for a moment. Go ahead. I’ll wait.)

  • Be quiet.
  • Watch patterns.
  • Talk less.
  • Don’t fuss.
  • Be watchful…of inaction.
  • Listen less.
  • Don’t diagnose it.
  • Don’t excuse it.
  • Observe it. Take notes if you have to.

Eventually, what people repeatedly do becomes louder than what they repeatedly say. So, stop explaining away what you see because it doesn’t match what you hoped for.

There is no expiration date on discernment, Sis.

Listen with your eyes. And, believe what you see.

Live Whole. Live Seen. Live Free.

Listen With Your Eyes…and Believe What You See

People will show you who they are. Pay attention—not with your ears, but with your eyes.

Renetta Smith, Founder & CEO, no more hiding™ · September 25, 2026 ·

We want to believe the people who say they love, care, and appreciate us, and so we close our eyes to what they consistently show us and keep listening to what they say. I’m guilty too. I believe the words…at first.

We ask for love, respect, promotion, priority—whether familial, platonic, sisterly, professional or romantic. We explain what love, care, appreciation, and respect look like to us, expecting it to be returned in a way we recognize, desire, and understand.

We have a duty to explain in words what kind of support we need in order to feel appreciated. We can’t assume our Boo knows our love language, or that our supervisor knows our planned path for development and advancement. We expect our besties, church family, and family members to observe our contributions and respond with love, care, and appreciation. But you can’t hold them accountable for meeting a need you have never communicated. Osmosis doesn’t count.

We have to explain it to them. For example, when communicating with our supervisor we may say something like:

Career advancement is important to me. I’d like us to discuss what advancement could realistically look like here, what I need to demonstrate, and what opportunities or milestones would indicate that I’m progressing toward it.

Examples of how I would communicate this at church or with family might sound something like:

I appreciate your prayers. Right now, though, I could really use some practical support too. If someone could bring dinner one evening, sit with Mom for a couple of hours, or just call and check on me occasionally, that would mean a lot.

Please keep sending those silly texts. They really cheer me up, but I also need to spend time with you. I miss you. Let’s decide on a date for lunch and a spa day.

Appreciation, love, and care can be expressed in many ways. There is no one-size-fits-all expression. But when someone knows what makes us feel loved, valued, or appreciated and repeatedly chooses not to show up in ways we can recognize, that pattern matters.

And sometimes, timing matters too. Love and appreciation repeatedly expressed too late can begin to feel like an afterthought—and over time, leave us feeling underappreciated. Because expressing love, appreciation, and care isn’t only about what you do. Sometimes, when you do it matters just as much.

The love, care, and appreciation they tell us we have doesn’t align with our personal definition of love…but we shut our eyes tighter…and keep listening.

We continue investing while explaining away what we’re seeing.

Decades…

Still feeling unheard, unappreciated, and unloved…but still listening.

Still waiting. Still hoping. Still giving.

Still tolerating.

The eyes don’t lie, Sis.

Be watchful. Listen less. Don’t diagnose it. Don’t excuse it. Observe it. Take notes if you have to.

Trust what you see as real evidence. Stop explaining away what you see. Stop justifying what they repeatedly do—or don’t do.

Words without actions eventually lose their weight.

Inaction. The evidence isn’t always what somebody did to you. Sometimes the evidence is what they never did for you at all. Pay attention when they don’t act.

People will show you how much they love and care about you without you having said a single word. When someone really loves and cares about you, they listen when you speak, they listen to the tone in which you speak and watch carefully when you don’t speak…anticipating your needs. Next thing you know, there’s a DoorDash delivery at the front door with your favorite ice cream, dental floss, nail file, nail clippers, toothpaste, retainer cleaner, toilet paper, paper towels, and your favorite bath soap.

Why?

Well… “Why?” could sound like a conversation between me and my sister:

Because I care about you and I care enough to listen when you speak… truth be told, I scribble notes when you talk to me so I don’t forget because it’s a fact I’m distracted most of the time. And I love you enough to anticipate that you could use a big spoon of your favorite ice cream and the rest of the stuff was simply because you probably needed it. I love you Sis…gotta run…got another meeting!

That’s what love looks like to me. And that’s how my sister interprets love and care. So, although I’m typically distracted, always talking fast because I’m always in a rush to dial into the next meeting… she knows I hear her (most of the time) and she knows I care enough to know her love language. Yes…sisters have a love language. LOL

Eventually, what people repeatedly do becomes louder than what they repeatedly say.

Sometimes we keep our eyes closed because opening them would force us to admit that the relationship we’ve been fighting to keep may not actually be the relationship we have. (Let that rest in your spirit for a moment.)

So, open your eyes. Stop explaining away what you see because it doesn’t match what you hoped for.

  • Watch the patterns.
  • Pay attention to the actions.
  • Pay attention when they don’t act.
  • Believe the evidence.
Ask Yourself

If I stopped listening to what they say and looked only at what they consistently do…would I still call this love, respect, appreciation or care?

There is no expiration date on discernment…so go ahead…open your eyes.

Listen with your eyes…and believe what you see.

Live Whole. Live Seen. Live Free.

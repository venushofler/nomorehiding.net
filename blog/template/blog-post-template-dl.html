Am I My Sister's Keeper?

Sisterhood alone does not entitle another woman to the privileges of friendship.

Renetta Smith, Founder & CEO, no more hiding™ · August 28, 2026 ·

Yes, I am my sister's keeper, but what I had to learn was where my sisterly responsibility ends and my sister's personal responsibility begins.

Sisterhood and friendship aren't the same thing. It took me 50 years to learn the difference and how to position these two relational concepts to establish boundaries and restore my peace.

Forgiveness can restore peace without restoring access.

What does sisterly commitment actually require?

Because now we're not talking about cutting everybody off every time they disappoint us. That's not discernment either. We're talking about paying attention to what the relationship has shown us.

Keep in Mind

Forgiveness, reconciliation, trust, and access are four separate decisions. Forgiveness is something you can choose for your own peace. Access is yours to give.

I Can Be My Sister's Keeper Without Becoming Her Caretaker

I believe I have a responsibility toward my sisters. However, I no longer believe that responsibility requires me to take responsibility for my sisters.

Our Story

Midlife isn't a crisis.
It's a crossroad.

no more hiding™ was founded in 2024 by Renetta Smith, born from a simple realization: Midlife isn't a crisis. It's a crossroad.

After spending decades devoted to motherhood, career, and responsibility, Renetta realized she had slowly disappeared from her own life. Her journey back to joy — through therapy, creativity, movement, and community — revealed something profound: countless women were quietly navigating the same midlife transitions, reinventions, and questions of identity.

What began as a few intimate gatherings with women who shared similar experiences quickly grew into something bigger. Renetta recognized a widespread need for accessible, relatable spaces where women could reconnect with themselves and each other — without judgment, pressure, or pretense.

no more hiding™ blends personal development, healthy lifestyle practices, physical movement, and self‑discovery with the power of community. Through small‑group gatherings, workshops, seminars, social events, and networking experiences, we create safe, inspiring environments where women can reflect, heal, laugh, cry, move, and grow.

Our approach is simple: Connection fuels clarity. Joy requires intention. And the best version of you was never meant to be hidden.

At any age. At any stage.

You deserve to live joyfully, authentically, and fully seen.

no more hiding™ is here to walk that journey with you.

Our Mission

A trusted resource for
every season of womanhood.

no more hiding™ exists to be a trusted resource for women — especially in midlife and post-retirement — navigating life's transitions with clarity, confidence, and courage.

Through reflective content, intentional gatherings, practical tools, and supportive spaces, we help women reconnect with their sense of joy, purpose, and self-trust.

Rooted in authenticity, compassion, and community, no more hiding™ empowers women to show up fully and unapologetically — honoring their lived experiences while moving forward with connection, courage, and joy.

Our Vision

Live whole. Live seen.
Live free.

A world where women — especially in midlife and post-retirement — live whole, live seen, and live free.

A world where women no longer shrink, silence themselves, or apologize for their growth — but instead choose clarity, confidence, and courage in every season of life.

no more hiding™ envisions women reclaiming their voices, honoring their lived experiences, and stepping forward without fear, shame, or self-erasure — emotionally, socially, professionally, and personally.

"Hiding is exhausting. Being seen is terrifying.
But living free? That's what we're after."

— Renetta Smith, Founder & CEO of no more hiding™
Meet Renetta

She's been where
you are right now.

no more hiding™ was created from a simple belief: every woman in midlife and beyond deserves a place where she can pause, reconnect with herself, and begin living more intentionally.

For years, Renetta listened as women quietly carried the weight of caregiving, careers, relationships, and life's many transitions — appearing to have everything together while privately feeling unheard, unseen, and disconnected from themselves.

Rather than creating another self-help program, Renetta envisioned something different — a welcoming community where women in midlife and beyond could find sanctuary, have honest conversations, and rediscover what makes them feel joyful and alive.

With more than 30 years of experience in project management, regulatory compliance, technology, and transportation infrastructure, supported by an MBA in International Business Management, Renetta combines strategic thinking with genuine compassion. Her greatest passion is helping women recognize that midlife is not a crisis — it's a crossroad. An invitation to repurpose our energy on living whole, living seen, and living free.

I Was the Woman
Hiding in Plain Sight.

For years, I showed up — at work, at church, in relationships — and never let anyone see the real me. I was living a life that looked full but felt empty.

Then I discovered the power of authentic community, of golf, of finally letting myself be seen. no more hiding™ is the movement I built for every woman who knows she was made for more — but hasn't given herself permission to go get it.

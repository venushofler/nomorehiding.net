At no more hiding™, we believe healing, growth, and support happen through connection. We're proud to highlight local businesses, professionals, and organizations that serve our community and share our commitment to helping women live whole, live seen, and live free. The businesses featured on this page are organizations that have positively impacted members of the no more hiding™ community through support, encouragement, expertise, and genuine care.

Every partner featured here represents a different pathway to healing, growth, connection, and living fully. Whether you're looking for wellness resources, personal services, community experiences, or trusted local professionals, we encourage you to explore the businesses featured below.

Sports & Hobbies

Joy of Golf

If you're considering trying golf and aren't sure where to start, start with Joy. Golf can feel intimidating, especially for beginners. Joy provides a down-to-earth, no-judgment approach that helps golfers of all skill levels feel comfortable learning the game. As a Certified Club Fitter at the PGA TOUR Superstore in Kensington, Maryland, Joy helps clients understand what equipment they need, how clubs are used, and how to select the right tools based on their goals, experience level, and budget. When you're ready to learn the rules of golf, understand basic golf etiquette, build confidence on the course, or determine whether golf is right for you, Joy offers practical guidance that makes the game more approachable and enjoyable. Whether you're picking up a club for the first time or returning to the game after years away, Joy's passion for helping others discover golf makes her a valuable resource for women exploring new experiences and rediscovering what makes them feel alive.

Owner: Joy Royes, Certified Club Fitter

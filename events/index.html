Friendsgiving @ The Winery has been postponed

Sometimes plans change, but the reason we gather never does. ❤️

Our Friendsgiving @ The Winery, originally scheduled for Saturday, November 28, 2026, has been postponed. After coordinating with the winery, we’ve secured the opportunity to reschedule our gathering for a later date. We’re looking forward to bringing everyone together for an afternoon filled with gratitude, connection, sisterhood, wonderful food, and great wine!

✨ A new date will be announced!

If you’ve been looking forward to sharing this special afternoon with us, don’t lose your excitement — we’re still looking forward to celebrating with you.

Refunds will be issued by the end of this week. Thank you for your understanding, patience, and continued support. Stay connected, and watch for our updated event announcement!
